# Ideas: what Oscar could also do online

These are proposals for Dinis and Oscar. None is on the site as a claim. The Interview page asks Oscar which he wants.
The site is static (no server, no cookies, no tracking, no third-party scripts), so anything that needs a booking, payment or video call is a link out to a service Oscar chooses. The site stays the shop window.

| Idea | What it is | What the site needs | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| Video-call lessons | One-to-one or small-group coaching over a video call: movement, fitness, tactics talk, planning | A "Book online" link to his chosen booking page; a plain-English "how it works" section | Low |
| Video feedback | A player sends a recorded match or drill; Oscar replies with notes or a short video | A "Send a clip" instruction (message him on Instagram, or an email he chooses); a clear line on turnaround that Oscar sets | Low |
| Remote performance plan | A written or video training plan for padel performance, to do at home or in a gym | A plan page per offer, with what is included | Medium |
| Drill library | Short drill and gym videos on a page, free or for members | Oscar's own videos, hosted by him; links out rather than embeds | Medium |
| Online group sessions | Live group warm-ups or movement classes for a club or friends | A schedule page (Oscar's own dates), a join link | Medium |
| Club and school packages | Remote check-ins for a club's players or juniors | A page for clubs, only with Oscar's words and the club's agreement | Medium |
| Newsletter or short tips | A monthly tip list from his own posts | A sign-up link to a service Oscar picks; no tracking on the site | Low |
| Fundraising page | The fundraising from his highlights, with a link to give | Oscar's words, the cause's own giving link, and thanks only with consent | Low |
| Reviews | Quotes from players | Only real, consented quotes in the player's own words. None are invented | Low |

Recommended first three: video-call lessons, video feedback, and the fundraising page. They need the least from Oscar and add the most to a first visit.
Reuse for other coaches: the same data file and designs fit any padel coach; the colours come from the `theme` in site/data/coach.json.
Not for now: prices, payments and a timetable, until Oscar decides.
