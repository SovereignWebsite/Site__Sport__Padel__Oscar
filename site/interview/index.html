Interview questions for Oscar

For Dinis Cruz to ask Oscar, so that Oscar can extend this site himself. Nothing here is on the site until Oscar has answered and agreed.

Not on the site yet: full name, where he coaches, qualifications, prices, availability, booking, testimonials, other people in photos.

1. Where you coach

  • Do you coach at one place or several? Please name them.
  • Are you happy for the site to say where you coach, and may it name the club?
  • Do you travel to coach, or coach at private courts?

2. Levels and groups

  • Who do you coach: beginners, improvers, advanced players, juniors, adults?
  • Do you offer one-to-one, pairs, small groups, or all of these?
  • Do you run clinics, camps or classes? How would you describe each?

3. Padel performance and personal training

  • What does a padel performance session involve, in your own words?
  • What does personal training with you involve, and who is it for?
  • Do you combine court and gym work for the same person?

4. Prices and booking

  • Do you want prices on the site, or only on request?
  • How do you want people to book: message you, a booking page, through a club?
  • Do you want to take payments through the site, or not?

5. Online and remote

  • Would you like to offer anything online, such as video-call lessons, video feedback on someone's recorded match, or a remote training plan?
  • Would you record short drill or fitness videos for the site, and who would appear in them?
  • If you offer online sessions, how should people book and pay: a link to a booking page, or a message to you?
  • Is there anything you would not want to do online?

6. Availability

  • Which days and times do you usually coach?
  • Should the site show a timetable, or just say "message to ask"?

7. Achievements and qualifications

  • Which coaching qualifications do you hold? Please give each exact name.
  • Are there results, rankings or playing history you want mentioned?
  • Is there anything you do not want mentioned?

8. Fundraising

  • What fundraising have you done, and for which cause?
  • Do you want a page for it, and a link where people can give?
  • Who should be thanked by name, and have they agreed?

9. Photos and video

  • Which photos and videos do you want on the site?
  • Has everyone who appears in them agreed to be on your site?
  • Should any club names or logos in the pictures be cropped out?
  • Your Instagram has Food, Gym and Fundraising highlights. Do you want a page for any of them?

10. Your name and how people reach you

  • Do you want your full name on the site?
  • Which contact method do you want: Instagram messages, WhatsApp, email, phone?
  • Is there a line or short story about you that you would like at the top of the home page?
